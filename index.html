About me

The operator.

Name's Justin but I usually go by JJ in the field. I grew up knowing one day I would have the impossible task of filling my father's shoes, so I got my first job at 14 years old. Ended up having to get a work permit from the state so the company couldn't get accused of breaking child labor laws.

Remained a blue collar worker until about May of '26. Always manual labor, always long hours, always unappreciated. About half of my working life was anything from making ice cream cones at Dairy Queen to painting cars and everything in between.

I eventually had a friend get hired on by a subcontracting company to do pump/valve replacements at power plants. I received a call from him and was made aware that the pay there was substantially better than my current profession so I hit the road.

The plants were hot, the hours were long, there were a million regulations, and you were almost always assured to have some brown-nosing bitch that no one could stand as your shift lead. By the end of my career on the road I was a top level precision tech working for the biggest name in gas turbines.

I've worked in one of the most physically/mentally draining heavy industrial jobs in the US for over a decade. We work 7/12's rain or shine for months at a time doing the most physically taxing jobs imaginable. I was sometimes only home for a month or two for the entire year. I've missed more birthdays, holidays, family emergencies, etc. than I'd even care to admit but, brother, nothing could've prepared me for the toll this shit has taken on me.

The mission

Why this channel exists

I spent ten years on the road as a gas turbine technician. During that time I also learned how to trade. Or so I thought.

In May of 2026, I decided I'd take the leap and try to trade full time. Going from a 7/12s schedule to working from home, where nobody sets a start time or tells you when to walk away.

Never in my life did I think that sitting at a desk all day could do what it does to someone.

Yes, being a precision tech that could potentially destroy a 10 million dollar machine if your measurements are written down with the decimal one place over in the wrong direction is pressure, but there is a safety net behind you to review everything. You might get your ass chewed but the machine still ends up running smoothly.

Not like that here. You fail and it's all on you. And typically it starts as a small fail so you figure you just missed that one. Next time. Then fail again. So you start to lose money. Then you make a couple good trades for small returns. So the next thought in your mind is, "Well if I was to double my position size I'll make double the profit"...

You'll read this and think, "fuck.. yeah I was there". Well I was too. I've ended up losing around 50 thousand dollars in the last six months or so. I've nearly maxed out all my credit cards. All my 401k is gone.

I've risked everything I have because I made a promise to myself ten years ago that I wanted this ONEEEE thing in my life and although I had convinced myself that I'm far too unintelligent to be able to pull it off, I can't allow myself to give up.

I am making this channel to give absolute full transparency of my trading days. The real P&L, the rules I know I need to follow yet still end up breaking anyways. The orders will be documented and automatically posted daily without any requirements or needed permissions of mine prior to the uploads.

And I just wanna say this.. I've had a lot of fear and gained a lot of courage that I didn't think I'd have to be willing to put myself out there like this but this is from me, as a man, to you.... I need this. Far beyond the money, I need this for me.. To finally prove to myself that I AM capable. I AM smart enough. I WILL achieve this. Or I'll fuckin lose everything I've worked so hard for my entire life trying...

Pre-start checklist

Before any order goes in

Pre-start check, same as a turbine. One box unticked, the unit doesn't run. The unit is me.

  • 01

    Card written

    Why, entry, stop, target and size on paper first. No card, no trade.

  • 02

    Risk under 1%

    If the stop hits, the account loses less than 1%.

  • 03

    Size under 25%

    No single position bigger than a quarter of the account.

  • 04

    Plain stop working

    A market stop, not a stop-limit, so an overnight gap can't skip it.

  • 05

    Earnings checked

    No new stock position within about two weeks of a report.

  • 06

    Head clear

    Slept, fed, not pissed off. Otherwise walk away from the desk.
